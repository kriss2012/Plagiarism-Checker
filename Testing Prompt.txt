==================================================
37. FIXING RULES
==================================================

When you identify a problem:

1. Reproduce it.
2. Identify the root cause.
3. Explain why it happens.
4. Fix the root cause.
5. Retest the original scenario.
6. Test adjacent functionality.
7. Test responsive behavior.
8. Confirm that the fix did not create a regression.

Do NOT apply temporary hacks.

Do NOT simply add:

overflow: hidden;

to hide a problem.

Do NOT reduce font size excessively to make content fit.

Do NOT hide important information.

Do NOT hardcode one viewport size.

Do NOT fix desktop at the expense of mobile.

Do NOT fix mobile at the expense of desktop.

Use maintainable responsive CSS.
